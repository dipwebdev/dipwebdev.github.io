Privacy Policy

Dip Web Dev ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Unit Converter Pro mobile application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personal data or usage analytics from our users. Unit Converter Pro is a fully native, offline application designed to respect your privacy.

Permissions Explained

In order to provide our core features, the app requests the following device permissions:

  • Camera & Flashlight: Required to access the device's LED flash to act as a flashlight. We do not take photos, record videos, or access your image gallery.
  • Foreground Service: Required to detect the physical shake motion of your device even when the app is minimized or the screen is locked. This ensures the flashlight can be activated instantly in emergencies.
  • Vibrate: Used to provide haptic feedback when the flashlight turns on or off via shaking.
  • Notifications: Required by Android to display a persistent notification while the foreground service is active, ensuring you are aware the app is running in the background.

Data Security

Since we do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on our servers, your data is inherently secure. All application data (such as user preferences or custom settings) is stored locally on your device using secure Android DataStore and Room databases.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
