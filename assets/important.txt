Core Feature Description: The core functionality of "Shake Flashlight Pro" allows users to turn on their device's camera flashlight by physically shaking their phone. This is a critical utility for emergency situations where the user needs immediate light without unlocking their phone or navigating through UI menus.

Why a Foreground Service is required: To detect the physical shake motion while the device screen is off or the app is closed, the app must maintain an active listener on the device's hardware Accelerometer sensor. Standard background processes are severely restricted by Android's battery management and will kill the sensor listener. Therefore, a Foreground Service (with a persistent, visible notification to the user) is the only technically viable way to ensure the accelerometer is monitored continuously and reliably for the user's safety.
