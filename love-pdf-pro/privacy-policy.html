Privacy Policy

Dip Web Dev ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Love PDF Pro mobile application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personal data or usage analytics from our users. Love PDF Pro is a fully native, offline application designed to respect your privacy.

Permissions Explained

In order to provide our core document processing features, the app requests the following device permissions:

  • All Files Access / Manage External Storage: Required to seamlessly access, organize, and edit PDF documents across your device's storage. Love PDF Pro needs this to function as a fully-featured document manager and editor.
  • Camera: Required specifically for the "Scan PDF" feature to allow you to digitize physical documents into PDFs. We do not access your camera outside of this specific tool.

Data Security & Offline Processing

Love PDF Pro is designed with privacy as its foundation. All document processing is performed 100% offline and locally on your device. Your sensitive PDFs, images, and scanned documents are never uploaded to any remote server or third-party cloud. The Machine Learning (ML) models used for OCR text extraction run entirely on your local hardware.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
